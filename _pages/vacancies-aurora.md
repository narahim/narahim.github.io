We are seeking part-time student assistants for **grant writing for Project Aurora** from **October 2026 to January 2027**. Open to **current UCC students only**, preferably **PhD students**.

Applicants should have a **strong background in wireless networks and computer science**, with good design, document formatting, and technical drawing skills. **Previous grant-writing experience is mandatory.**

Payment will be at **UCC's standard student help rate**, in accordance with UCC policy.

To express your interest, email [m.rahim@cs.ucc.ie](mailto:m.rahim@cs.ucc.ie?subject=Project%20Aurora%20%E2%80%93%20Grant-Writing%20Assistant), briefly stating your student status and previous grant-writing and formatting experience.
